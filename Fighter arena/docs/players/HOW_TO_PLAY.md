# How To Play

## Core controls

- `WASD`: move
- Double-tap `W`: sprint
- `Shift`: slide
- `Space`: jump
- `Mouse 1`: attack
- `Mouse 2`: aim
- `R`: reload
- `H`: heal
- `V`: first-person / third-person
- `Esc`: pause

## Weapon slots

- `1`: Minigun
- `2`: Carbine
- `3`: Shotgun
- `4`: Knife
- `5`: Flamethrower
- `6`: Bomb
- `7`: Shovel

The shovel can dig dirt terrain. In multiplayer, those terrain edits now sync with the room.

## Modes

### Single Player
Best place to learn movement, aiming, terrain digging, and weapons.

### Zombie Mode
Fight zombies, manage health, and survive pressure.

### Story Mode
Play chapter-based missions on more handcrafted maps.

### Multiplayer PvP
Create or join a room and fight other players.

### Multiplayer Horde
Fight zombies with friends while sharing the same arena.

### Mini Games
Short party-style challenges for solo or group play.

## Beginner advice

- Start in **Single Player** first.
- Learn `1-7` weapon swapping early.
- Use `7` to dig cover or ramps with the shovel.
- Press `J` if you want to reopen the in-game Beginner Guide.
